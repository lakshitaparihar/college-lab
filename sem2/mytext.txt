The wind moved slowly across the old stone road, and the road remembered every step. A quiet echo of footsteps,
footsteps that once meant something, something bright, something uncertain. The lantern beside the gate flickered twice,
twice like a small signal in the dark. Someone had scratched a tiny symbol on the wood: ∆, and beneath it another mark: ∞. 
Nobody knew why.
Morning came softly. The sky was pale blue, pale like paper waiting for words. Words repeat sometimes. Words return. Words 
wander and come back again. The road, the road, the road kept stretching forward like a long thought.
Somewhere a clock ticked: tick… tick… tick. Time is strange like that. Time circles. Time pauses. Time whispers in small 
signs: %, #, @. Nobody hears it clearly, but everyone feels it.
In the market square a chalkboard leaned against a wall. On it someone had written: “remember the quiet.” Then under it they drew small
patterns: ★ ★ ★ and a spiral that looked like @@@ curling inward. A child laughed, and the laugh echoed through the square like a 
bouncing symbol: ! ! !
Strange how small things repeat. The wind repeats. The road repeats. The word “maybe” repeats in the mind: maybe today, maybe tomorrow,
maybe again.
Far away, beyond the roofs and wires, the horizon shimmered like a line of code written by the sky:
{ sun + wind + silence } = day.
The lantern flickered again, again, again. And the symbol ∆ on the gate seemed to glow for just a moment, 
as if the night itself had written a quiet message nobody could fully read.
And still the road continued, the road continued, the road continued.
